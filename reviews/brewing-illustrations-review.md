# Teapots and brewing methods: illustration review

Prepared from main commit `8a5950c4b7e3fd8222902bd03f09984a33513782` (PR #50).

## Change

Six original, text-free SVG illustrations accompany the six existing kit rows on all three Tea labels pages. A native disclosure is initially open and can be collapsed without JavaScript. Existing introductory prose, tables, references, links, games and previous galleries are unchanged.

- Mug: separate bag and mesh-basket alternatives, explicitly captioned as cutaway views.
- English/Western pot: a larger sharing pot, distinguished from a kettle.
- Gaiwan: handleless bowl, raised lid and pictured saucer, without claiming the saucer is mandatory.
- Gongfu-style kit: both small clay and porcelain examples, paired with small cups.
- Grandpa style: loose leaves retained directly in a glass, with a water top-up.
- Matcha: powdered green tea in a bowl and a bamboo whisk with fine tines.

Captions and image alternatives are in English, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese. The introductory note explains that these are examples, not fixed designs or brewing steps, and are not drawn to a common scale. There are no added temperatures, times, ceremony instructions, health claims or universal cultural claims. The illustrations identify familiar vessel types without making provenance or universal-design claims. A reviewer removed an unsupported cotton-string qualifier from the two Chinese alt texts.

The six SVGs total 23,702 bytes. They contain no scripts, embedded raster images, external references or text requiring a fourth translation source. Art is deterministic and generated by `reviews/draw_brewing_art.py`; localized markup is synchronized from `data/tea-brewing-illustrations.json` by `reviews/sync_brewing_illustrations.py`.

## Reproduce

1. `python reviews/draw_brewing_art.py`
2. `python reviews/sync_brewing_illustrations.py`
3. `quarto render`
4. `python reviews/test_brewing_illustrations.py`
5. For managed browser QA, use `python reviews/build_brewing_preview.py <absolute-preview-directory>` and the supported `sites-preview` workflow. Open `_brewing-qa.html`, run the checks, and inspect the actual-page frame. `_brewing-art.html` is a separate art contact sheet.

The six figures were reviewed privately and approved for publication. Browser-only QA pages remain under `reviews/` and are not part of the rendered website. Private-preview deployment files are managed separately and are not included in this release.
